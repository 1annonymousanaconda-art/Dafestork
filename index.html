    Road Freight Solutions

    Domestic · Regional · Cross-border

    At Stork Delivery Service, we provide comprehensive road freight solutions designed to meet the diverse transportation needs of businesses and individuals alike. As a trusted logistics partner, we recognise the vital role road transportation plays in connecting markets, facilitating commerce, and ensuring the timely movement of goods.

    Backed by an extensive transportation network, a highly experienced team, and an unwavering commitment to service excellence, we deliver reliable, efficient, and cost-effective road freight services tailored to the unique requirements of each client. Whether transporting goods across cities, regions, or borders, we ensure every shipment is handled with professionalism, precision, and care.

    Ocean Freight Solutions

    FCL · LCL · Oversized cargo

    Unlock the full potential of global cargo transportation with our premium Ocean Freight services. We offer seamless, dependable shipping solutions for businesses seeking efficient international freight movement.

    Whether you need to transport large-volume shipments, oversized cargo, or require a cost-effective alternative for long-distance logistics, our Ocean Freight services are tailored to meet your specific needs. With a focus on reliability, competitive pricing, and end-to-end support, we ensure your cargo reaches its destination safely, efficiently, and on schedule.

    Air Freight Solutions

    Time-critical · High-value

    When speed is critical, Stork Delivery Service offers dependable Air Freight solutions designed to ensure the rapid and secure transportation of your goods. Our air cargo services provide efficient domestic and international shipping options for time-sensitive, high-value, and urgent consignments.

    Through our network of trusted airline partners and logistics professionals, we coordinate every stage of the shipment process, ensuring timely delivery, real-time visibility, and seamless handling from departure to destination. Whether you are shipping commercial goods, personal effects, medical supplies, or specialised cargo, our air freight services deliver reliability and peace of mind.

    Storage & Warehousing Solutions

    Short-term · Long-term

    Stork Delivery Service provides secure and flexible storage solutions to support your supply chain and inventory management needs. Our warehousing facilities are designed to accommodate a wide range of goods, offering both short-term and long-term storage options.

    With organised inventory systems, secure premises, and professional handling procedures, we ensure your products remain safe, accessible, and ready for distribution whenever required. Our storage solutions help businesses optimise operations, reduce logistics challenges, and improve overall efficiency.

    📦

    Packaging Solutions

    Fragile · Commercial · Specialised

    Proper packaging is essential to protecting goods during transit. At Stork Delivery Service, we offer professional packaging services tailored to the nature and requirements of your shipment. Our team uses quality packaging materials and industry best practices to safeguard items from damage, contamination, and handling risks.

    From fragile products and commercial merchandise to oversized cargo and specialised equipment, we provide customised packaging solutions that enhance security and ensure your goods arrive in excellent condition.

    Important notes

    • All shipments are subject to applicable transportation regulations and customs requirements.
    • Packaging requirements may vary depending on the type, size, weight, and destination of the shipment.
    • Certain hazardous, restricted, or prohibited items may not be accepted for transportation.
    • Storage rates and freight charges are determined based on cargo specifications, storage duration, destination, and service requirements.
    • Customers are encouraged to declare the full value of their goods and obtain appropriate insurance coverage where necessary.
    • Delivery timelines may vary due to weather conditions, customs inspections, public holidays, or other unforeseen circumstances.
